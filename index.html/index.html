Build a Better Spring Break Plan

Start with your group's priorities

Before choosing a resort, decide how much everyone can spend, whether you want a lively or relaxed atmosphere, and which amenities matter most. Check the full cost of flights, transfers, meals, activities, and accommodations rather than comparing room prices alone.

Travelers should confirm current entry requirements, resort policies, transportation arrangements, and safety guidance before booking a real trip.
